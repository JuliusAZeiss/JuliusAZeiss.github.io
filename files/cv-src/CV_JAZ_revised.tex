\section{Profile}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.15em]
  \item I develop classical algorithms that make difficult quantum problems tractable. Combining group theory, quantum Shannon theory, and convex optimization, I use quantum de Finetti theorems and symmetry-reduced semidefinite programming hierarchies to obtain rigorous bounds on quantum correlations and quantum error correction. Alongside these algorithmic questions, I study the power and limitations of entanglement in communication tasks and non-local games, especially quantum graph coloring. My work connects quantum information with graph theory and combinatorics, investigating how entanglement changes classical constraints and how graph structure shapes quantum strategies.
  \item Speaker at leading international conferences (TQC, Beyond IID, AQIS) and invited speaker at institutions including Stanford University and the Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics
  \item Host of a regular A.I.\ Workshop at RWTH Aachen on AI tools for rigorous, reproducible science
\end{itemize}
