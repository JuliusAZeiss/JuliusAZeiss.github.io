**Abstract.** De Finetti theorems are a central tool for turning symmetry into efficient algorithms, but in constrained optimization problems their power has so far fallen short of the unconstrained case. In this talk, I will present a family of new information-theoretic de Finetti theorems that bridge this gap for constrained separability and related optimization problems. The main results give convergence rates comparable to the best unconstrained bounds, introduce a Bose-symmetric theorem that supports efficient symmetry-reduced semidefinite programs, and reveal a new interpolation between classical and quantum de Finetti phenomena. A key feature of the approach is that it is constructive: besides outer relaxations, it also produces certified inner approximations via a rounding procedure with explicit error control. I will then discuss two applications, namely polynomial-time approximation of fixed-size quantum games and improved finite-blocklength coding bounds in quantum Shannon theory.
