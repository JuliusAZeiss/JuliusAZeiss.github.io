**Abstract.** We develop quantitative de Finetti representation theorems beyond the standard quantum setting, guided by the principle that permutation symmetry enforces approximate independence at finite extension level. Using a GPT-inspired relative entropy defined through an integral representation, we introduce mutual information for general convex state spaces and prove a uniform monogamy bound for multipartite extensions: for every permutation-invariant state, the total mutual information I(A:B₁⋯Bₙ) is bounded by a constant depending only on the A-system. This yields an information-theoretic finite de Finetti theorem for convex bodies. Specifically, the AB-marginal of an n-extendible state in the maximal tensor product is within O(n^(−1/2)), in a natural base/order-unit norm, of a separable state in the minimal tensor product. The resulting convergence rate is independent of the dimension of the full extension.

We then turn to constrained separability problems in quantum information. We establish constrained de Finetti theorems that preserve prescribed linear marginal and fixed-point constraints, together with a Bose-symmetric variant formulated directly for extensions supported on the symmetric subspace. These results provide quantitative trace-norm approximations of constrained symmetric — and Bose-symmetric — extensions by convex mixtures of product states whose components satisfy the corresponding local constraints. They therefore extend de Finetti control to settings in which one cannot rely on extremal decompositions into pure product states.
